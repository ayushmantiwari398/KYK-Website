Help & Support

How to search products?

Use the search box on the Home page or Products page and type a product name or category.

How to add products to cart?

Open a category or Products page and click the Add to Cart button.

How to update your profile?

Click Profile in the top navigation, fill in your details and click Save Profile.

How to become a seller?

Click Register Your Shop on the Home page and submit the form.

For other questions, visit the Contact page.
